### What this fixes * **No image cropping** — `object-fit: contain` keeps the complete photo visible. * **Each product keeps the same image-box size.** * **Multiple images don't overlap.** * **`‹` and `›` work independently for each product.** * Counter changes: `1 / 3 → 2 / 3 → 3 / 3`. * On the last image, clicking next returns to the first. * On the first image, clicking previous goes to the last. * It works without relying on `DOMContentLoaded` or attaching JavaScript listeners to every button. One thing to note: because your photos may have different proportions, **some empty background around a photo is normal with `contain`**. That's what prevents the actual clothing/product from being cut.
